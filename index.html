About International Consortium of Empirical Analysis

We are an independent, non-governmental institution dedicated to uncovering practical solutions to complex research problems. Grounded in rigorous research methodologies—including surveys, questionnaires, and strategic project implementation—we transform empirical data into actionable outcomes.

Leadership & Background: Founded by a distinguished research expert with extensive experience across the sector, including notable collaborations with prominent organizations such as BRAC and WAN JEM. To ensure the highest standards of transparency, reliability, and justice in all institutional operations, the founder partnered with a leading human rights expert. This partnership guarantees that our research remains inclusive, ensuring no segment of the target population is left out. Under a formal partnership agreement, this expert serves as co-founder, jointly guiding the professional execution of our vision and mandate.

Vision & Mandate: The vision and mandate of the International Consortium of Empirical Analysis are deeply rooted in global trust, reliability, and transparency.
